Self-hosted typefaces — licence record
======================================

Spec §5 Type names three faces. All three are licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits commercial use, embedding,
and redistribution as part of a bundled software/web project, provided
the font files are not sold on their own and the copyright notice below
travels with them. No attribution is required in the rendered page.

Files were subset by Google Fonts (latin + latin-ext) and fetched on
2026-09-03 from fonts.gstatic.com. They are served by next/font/local
from this directory — not from public/ — so Next.js fingerprints them
and emits the @font-face rules itself. No runtime request leaves the
origin; see spec §5 "Self-host via next/font".
